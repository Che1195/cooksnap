## Method

Provider: `muse-spark-1.3`, low reasoning, prompt `capture-2-meta`, standard tier. Loaded only the selected CookSnap key into the evaluation process. No credentials appear in these artifacts.

Ran the existing 20 measurement fixtures and 20 balanced fixtures. Four balanced cases model rejection without inference. Four usable sources repeat between suites: 36 baseline AI captures represent 32 distinct analyzable synthetic sources. Each capture had a 25-second deadline and at most two generation attempts. Two suite processes ran concurrently, with sequential cases within each suite.

These are hand-authored/adapted sources. Simulated rendered snapshots bypass rendering. Rejection fixtures simulate status/content checks. No publisher fetching, real browser rendering, application UI, production admission, or persistence was exercised.

The initial collector rejected nullable provider envelopes, losing raw final answers and transport-level usage. Accepted application outputs, projections, cumulative application telemetry, HTTP status, and timing were retained. The collector was corrected before targeted repeats. Original files remain unchanged.

## Open findings

1. **Observed title omission.** Visible-source validation checks returned nonempty facts for source inclusion, but does not ensure source facts were retained (`src/lib/recipe-capture-model.ts:210`). Add source-completeness handling and regression evidence before release.
2. **Observed deadline pressure.** Two tiny fixtures failed at 25 seconds; one successful repeat took 23.5 seconds. Investigate latency and validation retries within the existing route deadline. Do not claim raising a timeout resolves quality.
3. **Reproduced scaling gap, not observed in live accepted outputs.** Valid metadata with instruction references and `quantities: []` leaves `Melt 2 tbsp butter.` unchanged at 2× while ingredient `4 tbsp butter` becomes `8 tbsp butter`, with no warning. Without metadata the instruction becomes `Melt 4 tbsp butter.` Main and reviewer independently reproduced this. Instruction projection honors the empty array at `src/lib/recipe-interpretation.ts:523`; ingredient projection regenerates quantities at line 490. Resolve completeness or warning behavior.

Low-priority wording issue: doubling `1 red onion` displays `2 red onion`; numeric behavior is correct.
